Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before starting.

Does it work without internet?
Yes. HuluStock caches data locally and continues working even when your connection drops. Sales, scanning and inventory all work offline. Data syncs automatically when you're back online.
Can I use it for multiple branches?
Absolutely. You can manage all your branches from one dashboard. Each branch has its own stock, users and sales data but you see everything in one place.
Can I print receipts?
Yes. HuluStock supports receipt printers. You can auto-print after every sale or print on demand.
Can I import my existing products?
Yes. We can import your products from Excel or CSV during onboarding. Just send us your file and we'll handle it.
Can multiple employees use it?
Yes. Create separate accounts for each employee with role-based permissions (admin, manager, cashier). Each person sees only what they need.
Does it support pharmacies?
Yes. HuluStock has a dedicated pharmacy mode with batch tracking, FEFO (first-expiry-first-out), expiry alerts, packaging tiers and controlled substance management.
Can I use it on mobile?
Yes. HuluStock is a progressive web app (PWA) that works on any device with a browser — phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. You can also install it on your home screen.
